# TC-0049 — A story is built, reviewed beside its criteria, and accepted

Verifies [RQ-0015](../requirements/rq-0015.md) AC-1 to AC-5 through the running application,
against the stub agent's `file-writer` mode — which makes a scripted change to a source file and
ends its turn. Asserted: the walk, the surfaces, and Git left unwritten.

## Steps

1. Press Build on a ready story. Expect it `building` on the board and the conversation showing
   the turn.
2. When the turn ends, expect the story at `review` and the review surface holding its criteria
   beside the diff of what the stub changed.
3. Send it back with a note. Expect `building`, the note on the story, and a new turn carrying
   it; let that turn end.
4. Accept. Expect `accepted` on disk — and expect `git log` unchanged and the change still
   uncommitted in the working tree.
