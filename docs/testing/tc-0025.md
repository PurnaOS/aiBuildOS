# TC-0025 — Acceptance criterion numbers are append-only

Verifies [RQ-0005](../requirements/rq-0005.md) AC-7.

The number is the identity. Elsewhere in the bundle a criterion is referred to as `RQ-0007#AC-2`, and
a TestCase may verify that one criterion rather than the whole artifact
([OKF conventions §3](../guidelines/okf-conventions.md)) — so renumbering after a deletion silently
repoints every one of those references at different text. That is why deleting retires a number
instead of closing the gap.

## Steps

1. Read a criteria section, including a criterion wrapped across two lines. Expect one entry each,
   the wrapped one joined.
2. Read a body with no criteria section. Expect none, not an error.
3. Delete a middle criterion and write back. Expect the ones after it to keep their numbers, and the
   deleted number to appear nowhere.
4. Write the criteria back unchanged. Expect every other part of the body untouched.
5. Write into a body with no criteria section. Expect the body returned unchanged.
