# TC-0025 — An artifact's body survives being edited

Verifies [RQ-0005](../requirements/rq-0005.md) AC-7 and AC-8, for the half of AC-8 that is not
frontmatter.

Two properties, both about not destroying what nobody touched. A criterion's **number** is its
identity: elsewhere in the bundle it is referred to as `RQ-0007#AC-2`, and a TestCase may verify that
one criterion rather than the whole artifact ([OKF conventions §3](../guidelines/okf-conventions.md)),
so renumbering after a deletion silently repoints every one of those references at different text.
And a criterion written across two lines — which is most of them in this repository — must come back
written across two lines, or a one-field edit becomes a diff over the whole document.

## Steps

1. Split a body with prose, criteria and a section after them. Expect the three parts, and a wrapped
   criterion read as one.
2. Join it back with nothing edited. Expect the body byte-identical.
3. Edit one criterion. Expect the others written back as the exact lines they arrived as, wrapping
   included.
4. Delete a middle criterion. Expect the ones after it to keep their numbers, and the deleted number
   to appear nowhere.
5. Append a criterion. Expect the ones above it untouched.
6. Append a criterion and write before anything is typed into it. Expect it written, not swallowed.
7. Split a body with no criteria section. Expect all of it treated as prose, and returned unchanged.
