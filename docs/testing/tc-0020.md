# TC-0020 — A live session's narration crosses the real boundary into a real renderer

Verifies [RQ-0004](../requirements/rq-0004.md) AC-3, AC-11 and AC-18. Playwright against the built
application ([DC-0013](../decisions/dc-0013.md)), with the harness pointed at the scripted stub.

[TC-0014](tc-0014.md), [TC-0015](tc-0015.md) and [TC-0016](tc-0016.md) each prove one part in
isolation, against fakes. This proves the parts are wired to one another through Electron, which no
in-memory fake can establish.

It drives the boundary directly rather than through a composer, because the interface that will do
that is [ST-0011](../user-stories/st-0011.md); what is under test here is the wiring beneath it.

## Steps

1. Launch with a project and two harnesses configured, both pointed at the stub.
2. Subscribe to session events in the renderer, then open a session on the project.
3. Send a prompt and wait for the turn to stop. Expect it to stop for an ordinary reason.
4. Expect the renderer to have received the turn's narration: its start, its message text, its
   reasoning, its tool call, its plan, and its end.
5. Expect none of it to carry the agent's own protocol vocabulary — the renderer receives AG-UI and
   nothing else.
6. Open a second session, prompt it, and cancel mid-turn. Expect the turn to end as cancelled rather
   than as a failure.
7. Close both sessions.
