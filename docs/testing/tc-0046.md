# TC-0046 — Approval flips what it may, and refuses what the record's rules refuse

Verifies [RQ-0014](../requirements/rq-0014.md) AC-3, AC-5 and AC-6 as logic: the derivation of
the plan view from a record, and the walk of flips an approval performs.

## Steps

1. Derive the plan from a record with drafts implementing two requirements, dependencies among
   them. Expect grouping by requirement and dependency-then-ID order.
2. Approve a plan of valid drafts. Expect a flip per artifact — stories to `ready`, test cases
   to `active` — each through the guarded edit.
3. Approve a plan holding one story with no verifying test. Expect that flip refused with the
   record's reason, the story still `draft`, and every other flip made.
