# TC-0044 — Playbooks are discovered from the record and composed with their context

Verifies [RQ-0013](../requirements/rq-0013.md) AC-1, AC-2 and AC-6 as functions: from a loaded
record to buttons, and from a playbook plus selected artifacts to the prompt text.

## Steps

1. From a record holding active and retired playbooks, derive the buttons. Expect one per active
   playbook, carrying the artifact's title, and none for the retired.
2. Compose a playbook with two selected artifacts. Expect the body verbatim plus a context line
   naming each selection — nothing invented, nothing hidden.
3. Resolve the harness for a playbook naming one by display name — matching, missing, and absent.
   Expect the match, the fallback, the fallback.
