# TC-0101 — Builds and background runs spend from one cap

Verifies [RQ-0039](../requirements/rq-0039.md) AC-2 at the unit level: `busyCount()` — busy
means `storyId ≠ null` or `label ≠ null` — is the single check both `build:start` and background
`session:start` consult, with `CONCURRENCY_CAP = 3` living beside it.

## Steps

1. Hold three busy sessions in a mix of kinds — builds carrying a `storyId`, background runs
   carrying a `label`. Expect a fourth start refused, whichever door it knocks on: `build:start`
   and background `session:start` both hit the cap.
2. Hold plain chat sessions — no `storyId`, no `label`. Expect them not counted: a full chat
   inventory leaves the cap untouched.
3. End one busy session. Expect the next start admitted.
