# TC-0097 — The build listing carries every worktree's facts

Verifies [RQ-0037](../requirements/rq-0037.md) AC-2 and AC-4 at the unit level: `listBuilds`
filling the new `BuildRow` fields — `path`, `sprintId`, `ahead`, `lastCheckpointAt` — and
classifying sprint worktrees into the `sprints` rows, against a fixture repo.

## Steps

1. List with a story worktree two commits ahead of its base. Expect its row carrying the
   worktree `path`, `ahead: 2` via `rev-list --count base..HEAD`, and `lastCheckpointAt` as the
   last commit's ISO date via `log -1 --format=%aI`.
2. List with a worktree that has no checkpoints yet. Expect `lastCheckpointAt` absent — a fact
   the dock can put into words, not a blank or an epoch.
3. List with a sprint worktree and a story building off it on `aibuildos/sp-NNNN--st-NNNN`.
   Expect the story row carrying its `sprintId`, and the response's `sprints` entry naming the
   sprint id, branch, path, dirty state, and that story.
4. Dirty the sprint worktree and list again. Expect its `dirty` flag flipped.
