# TC-0054 — An abandoned interview keeps its drafts, as drafts

Verifies [RQ-0017](../requirements/rq-0017.md) AC-4 and AC-5.

## Steps

1. Seed a draft already on the record, start a fresh interview, answer its first question, and
   stop before the second one is answered — the stub's writing turn is the third, and it never
   runs. Expect the seeded draft unchanged on the backlog, at `draft`, and the conversation still
   usable.
2. Seed an interview-shaped draft the validator rejects. Expect it marked where it appears and the
   rest of the record usable around it.
