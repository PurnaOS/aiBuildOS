# TC-0092 — A sprint starts from picked stories and finishes onto main

Verifies [RQ-0035](../requirements/rq-0035.md) AC-1, AC-3 and AC-5 through the running
application: the Work header's sprint affordances driving the lifecycle end to end.

## Steps

1. Check two ready stories on the Work board. Expect "Start a sprint with 2 stories"; press it.
   Expect the Sprint artifact in `docs/sprints/`, branch `aibuildos/sp-NNNN`, and its worktree
   on disk.
2. Expect the header's sprint selector filtering the board to the sprint's stories, with a
   progress count.
3. With a story worktree still alive, press Finish. Expect the refusal surfaced in
   `stories_live` terms — main unmoved.
4. Clear the story worktree and press Finish again. Expect a `--no-ff` merge commit on main and
   the sprint's worktree gone.
