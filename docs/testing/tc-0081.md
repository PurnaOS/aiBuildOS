# TC-0081 — The rail folds and counts; the chrome asks in its own voice

Verifies [RQ-0029](../requirements/rq-0029.md) AC-1 to AC-3 through the running application.

## Steps

1. Fold a group from its header; expect its rows gone, its count (a structural fact, not a filtered
   one) still shown, and the fold surviving a watcher-driven refresh.
2. Filter from the top; expect a match count ("N match"), one-press clear, and a folded group with a
   match to auto-unfold for as long as the filter is active — the fold itself is untouched
   underneath and resumes the moment the filter clears. (The alternative this story considered —
   leaving the group folded and only highlighting its count — was passed over as the less legible of
   the two honest options; see the comment in `RecordRail.tsx`.)
3. Close a dirty tab; expect the application's own dialog (`discard-dialog`/`discard-confirm`/
   `discard-cancel`), in the dark appearance too.
