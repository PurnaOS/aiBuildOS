# TC-0116 — Plan is browsable, and Build goes parallel

Verifies [RQ-0045](../requirements/rq-0045.md) AC-1, AC-2 and AC-4 through the running
application: the three surfaces hide no second strip, a drafted plan is reached by browsing
rather than by a banner's grace, and the Build button's first press is the parallel path.

## Steps

1. Walk Chat, Plan and Work. Expect the three pinned tabs and nothing else pinned, and no nested
   tab strip inside any of them.
2. Draft a plan, then browse to Plan from another surface. Expect the draft rendered in-surface
   with its review and edit affordances — and no conditional banner anywhere on the way.
3. Press the Build split button's primary action on a ready story. Expect a worktree build to
   start — a branch and worktree appear, not a prompt into the conversation — with
   "In this conversation" offered behind the caret.
