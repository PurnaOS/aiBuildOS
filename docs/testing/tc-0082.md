# TC-0082 — Streaming shows on the tab, and what changed says so

Verifies [RQ-0030](../requirements/rq-0030.md) through the running application against the slow
stub.

## Steps

1. During a slow turn on the MAIN session, expect the conversation tab to say it is working (a word
   and a quiet pulse), and to stop when the turn ends. A build session's own tab carries the same
   treatment while that session is `busy`, tracked independently in `now/motion.ts`.
2. Drive a worktree build with the file-writer stub; expect its Now row's activity in plain words
   (never `TEXT_MESSAGE_CONTENT` or the like — `describeActivity` in `now/now.ts`) with elapsed time
   since the last event, and the row naming that it works in its own copy, by branch. A regex for
   `[A-Z_]{6,}` guards against raw protocol vocabulary leaking into the row.
3. Change an artifact's `state` on disk, from outside the application; expect its rail row to say
   "changed", and the mark to clear on opening it. Honesty note: the mark is `RecordRail`'s own diff
   of its last two reads (state or title differs, or the id is new) — it is not the watcher naming
   what changed, since `project:changed` carries no paths. A content-only body edit is invisible to
   it; the mark also clears itself after ~15s if the row is never opened.
