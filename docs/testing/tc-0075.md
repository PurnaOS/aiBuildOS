# TC-0075 — The watcher debounces, filters, and caches

Verifies [RQ-0026](../requirements/rq-0026.md) AC-4, AC-6 and AC-7 at the watcher module and the
record cache, against real temp directories.

## Steps

1. A burst of writes yields one event after the debounce; a write under an ignored tree yields
   none; a write under `.git/refs` yields one.
2. Two record reads with nothing changed parse once; a change between them parses twice.
3. Stopping the watcher ends events; nothing leaks between projects.
