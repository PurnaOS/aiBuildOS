# TC-0091 — Sprint lifecycle honours its guards

Verifies [RQ-0035](../requirements/rq-0035.md) AC-1, AC-3 and AC-6 at the unit level:
`startSprint`, `mergeSprint` and `discardSprint` against a fixture repo, the `stories_live`
guard, and the `--` branch parse that lets a sprint be recovered from git alone.

## Steps

1. `startSprint` with picked story IDs. Expect the Sprint artifact minted with
   `contains: [ST-…]`, branch `aibuildos/sp-NNNN` created, and a worktree checked out on it.
2. With a story worktree alive on `aibuildos/sp-NNNN--st-NNNN`, call `mergeSprint` and
   `discardSprint`. Expect both refused with `stories_live`.
3. Remove the story worktree, `mergeSprint`. Expect a `--no-ff` merge commit on main.
4. Parse the branch names back: `aibuildos/sp-0001--st-0049` yields the sprint and the story;
   `aibuildos/sp-0001` yields the sprint alone — the whole binding recoverable from refs, no
   tracked file consulted.
