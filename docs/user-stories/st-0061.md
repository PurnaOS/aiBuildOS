# ST-0061 — Playbooks in the composer

*As someone starting work, I want playbooks and agent commands offered where I compose, so that
starting one is just another way of writing a message.*

Implements [RQ-0043](../requirements/rq-0043.md): PlaybookStrip and the COMMANDS chip die,
replaced by a `+` menu on the composer — a Playbooks section fed by `derivePlaybooks` and a
Commands section fed by the agent's advertised commands — plus centred starter cards when the
transcript is empty. The pure composition modules (`playbooks.ts`) do not change; only where their
output is offered does.

## Acceptance criteria

- [AC-1] The composer's `+` menu lists Playbooks from `derivePlaybooks` and Commands from the
  agent's advertised commands; choosing an entry composes exactly what the strip or chip composed.
- [AC-2] PlaybookStrip and the COMMANDS chip are deleted; an empty transcript shows the playbooks
  as centred starter cards, and picking a card starts that playbook.
- [AC-3] The intake playbook still opens the idea prompt before composing.
