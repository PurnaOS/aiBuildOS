# ST-0064 — Three surfaces

*As someone moving between talking, planning and building, I want the pinned tabs to be those
three acts, so that no surface hides a second tab strip inside itself.*

Implements [RQ-0045](../requirements/rq-0045.md): the pinned tabs become **Chat | Plan | Work**.
BoardTab's nested strip dies — Column and Card move to `boards/Column.tsx`, shared rather than
forked; Plan merges BacklogBoard with PlanTab into one surface (`boards/PlanSurface.tsx`), killing
the conditional banner that was the plan's only entry; Work is the board plus the sprint header.
The Build split button flips — primary is the worktree build, "In this conversation" behind the
caret — per the owner's decision in [DC-0027](../decisions/dc-0027.md); build.spec and commit.spec
migrate deliberately in the spec-migration slot.

## Acceptance criteria

- [AC-1] The pinned tabs are exactly Chat, Plan and Work; `TabKind` loses `now`; no pinned surface
  renders a nested tab strip.
- [AC-2] Browsing to Plan shows any drafted plan in-surface with its review and edit affordances;
  the conditional banner is gone; both boards render columns and cards from `boards/Column.tsx`.
- [AC-3] The Build split button's primary action starts a worktree build; "In this conversation"
  sits behind the caret; document tabs keep their preview-tab semantics unchanged.
