# ST-0024 — The backlog and the work are boards

*As someone steering more work than one artifact tab can show, I want the requirements and the
work laid out as boards whose columns are their states, so that the shape of everything is one
glance, and scheduling is one move.*

Serves [RQ-0011](../requirements/rq-0011.md).

A pinned Board tab beside Chat, with a Backlog view (Requirements) and a Work view (Stories and
Bugs) behind the same internal switcher the files rail uses. Cards derive from the loaded record;
column membership *is* the artifact's state; order inside a column is priority then ID, computed
every render and stored nowhere. A drag calls the same guarded artifact save the editor uses —
which is why this story follows [ST-0023](st-0023.md) — and every drag has a plain-words menu
twin. On the Work view, drop targets exist only for the verdicts a person owns; the builders'
columns are for watching, and say so.

## Acceptance criteria

- [AC-1] The Board tab is pinned and unclosable beside Chat, with Backlog and Work views;
  columns come from the profile's vocabularies.
- [AC-2] A card shows title, ID and priority, and opens its artifact as a tab; dragging it
  performs the state edit through the guarded save, with illegal drops refused in the refusal's
  own words and the card staying put.
- [AC-3] Each drag has a non-drag twin on the card, in plain words.
- [AC-4] The Work view offers drops only for `review` verdicts and retirement; the builders'
  columns take no drops and say why.
- [AC-5] The boards re-derive when the workspace refreshes, and identically after reopening the
  project.
