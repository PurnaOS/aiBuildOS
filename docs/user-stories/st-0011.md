# ST-0011 — The workspace shell: three panes and a tabbed centre

*As someone working in a project, I want the record, the conversation and the files on screen at once,
so I can see what the work is for and where it lands without leaving the conversation.*

Serves [RQ-0004](../requirements/rq-0004.md) criteria AC-1 and AC-2.

The ordering is the product's argument, not a preference: the record is on the left because it is what
the work is *for*, the files are on the right because that is where it *lands*, and the conversation
that connects them is in the middle.

This replaces the status page [ST-0005](st-0005.md) built. Its branch, working-tree counts and commit
list do not disappear — they move to the Git tab of the right pane, where they belong.

## Acceptance criteria

- [AC-1] A project opens into three panes: the record, the conversation, the files.
- [AC-2] Each side pane can be resized and collapsed to a labelled edge, and both widths survive a
  restart.
- [AC-3] The centre is a tab strip. The conversation is the first tab and has no close control.
- [AC-4] Opening something from either side pane opens it as a tab beside the conversation, and
  opening the same thing twice focuses the existing tab rather than opening a second.
- [AC-5] A single click opens a preview tab that the next preview replaces; a double click keeps it.
- [AC-6] The window opens large enough for three panes without the conversation being squeezed.
- [AC-7] Settings and the harness list stay reachable with a project open, because a harness belongs
  to the installation rather than to a project.
