# ST-0048 — Motion is visible

*As someone glancing at the workspace, I want to see that work is happening and what just moved,
so that quiet means quiet and not blind.*

Serves [RQ-0030](../requirements/rq-0030.md): the streaming word-and-pulse on the conversation's
tab, elapsed time and plain words on Now rows, the transient "changed" mark the watcher makes
possible on rail rows, and the worktree row saying where the build's files live.

## Acceptance criteria

- [AC-1] A streaming turn shows on its tab in a word and stops when the turn does.
- [AC-2] Now rows read in plain words with elapsed time; a worktree row names its own copy.
- [AC-3] A rail row that just changed says so, briefly, in a word.
