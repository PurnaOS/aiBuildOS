# ST-0035 — A manual test is walked, and the walk is written down

*As the person a checklist is for, I want the steps in front of me and one press at the end, so
that "I checked it" becomes a fact in the record instead of a memory.*

Serves [RQ-0023](../requirements/rq-0023.md). The TestCase profile gains three optional fields —
`last_result`, `last_run`, `last_run_by` — and the review surface renders a manual test case's
Steps as a walkable checklist: tick through in order, finish with pass or fail, and the fields
land through the guarded save. The engine's field editing has never written a TestCase before —
that path gets its own unit test before any surface exists.

## Acceptance criteria

- [AC-1] The profile (template included) declares the outcome fields as optional; absent means
  never walked.
- [AC-2] A story under review lists its manual test cases; opening one walks its Steps in order
  and finishing records result, time and walker through the guarded save.
- [AC-3] Abandoning a walk writes nothing; the latest outcome shows beside the automated checks.
