# ST-0036 — A changed requirement shows its blast radius, and offers a re-plan

*As someone whose mind changed, I want to see what my change touches — built, building, tested —
and hand the mess to the planner, so that nothing quietly falls out of date.*

Serves [RQ-0024](../requirements/rq-0024.md), outside the epic structure as that requirement
records. The impact is a derivation over links the record already stores: the artifact editor,
after saving a requirement past `ready`, shows its inbound `implemented_by` and `verifies` with
each artifact's state, grouped by done / in flight / verification; one button starts the plan
playbook with the changed requirement as context, exactly as a first plan does.

## Acceptance criteria

- [AC-1] Saving a change to a requirement past `ready` surfaces its implementers and verifiers
  with their states, told apart as done, in flight, and verification.
- [AC-2] One press starts the plan playbook with the changed requirement as context.
- [AC-3] Appending a criterion to a verified requirement leaves existing numbers intact, and
  nothing anywhere is demoted by machinery.
