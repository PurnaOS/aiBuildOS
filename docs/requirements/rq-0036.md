# RQ-0036 — Build resume

[DC-0021](../decisions/dc-0021.md) promised that a story with a live worktree "can be picked up by
a fresh session in that worktree" — and nothing ever implemented the pickup. After a quit, a crash,
or a session that simply ended, the worktree and its checkpoints survive but the only paths forward
are merge or discard. Resume closes the gap: a fresh session attaches to the surviving worktree and
continues from its checkpoints, sharing the exact attach path a started build uses.

## Acceptance criteria

- [AC-1] A story whose worktree survives — under either branch form, `aibuildos/st-NNNN` or
  `aibuildos/sp-NNNN--st-NNNN` — can be resumed: a fresh session attaches in that worktree,
  prompted from its checkpoints.
- [AC-2] Resume refuses with `not_found` when no worktree matches the story, `already_attached`
  when a session already runs there, and the cap refusal when three builds are busy.
- [AC-3] A resumed story at `review` walks back to `building`; from there checkpoints and the
  turn-end flip behave exactly as in a freshly started build.
- [AC-4] Resume works across an application relaunch — the worktree located by branch alone, no
  session state consulted.
