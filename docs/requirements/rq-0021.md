# RQ-0021 — Several builds run at once, and one place shows them all

The vision doc's step 7 at full strength: several pieces building at the same time, each agent in
its own worktree, and one surface that answers "what is happening right now" without opening
anything. That surface is the attention half of what the boards began — the boards show the
record's shape, this shows the work's motion, and a build waiting on a person is the one thing it
must make impossible to miss.

## Acceptance criteria

- [AC-1] More than one story builds at once, each agent in its own worktree, none sharing a
  working directory; the interface's concurrency cap is a stated ceiling, not a discovery.
- [AC-2] One pinned surface lists every running build — its story, its state, its latest
  activity — live, with each build's conversation one press away.
- [AC-3] A build waiting on a person is marked on that surface in words and count, visible from
  anywhere in the workspace, and answerable without entering the stream.
- [AC-4] Cancelling one build leaves the others running, and the boards show several cards at
  `building` as the normal picture.
