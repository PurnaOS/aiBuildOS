# RQ-0041 — No inert affordances

The zero-breakage slice of the redesign: everything that looks pressable becomes pressable, every
label says what its press does, and nothing advertises behaviour it does not have. The rail's
inbound links render as text a user keeps trying to click; PlanTab still blocks the renderer with
a native `window.confirm` — the one appearance [RQ-0029](rq-0029.md)#AC-3 did not reach, folded
here rather than minted as a bug; two components declare callbacks nothing passes; and two pairs
of near-identical buttons ("Commit"/"Commit", the review's two sends) leave the reader guessing
which is which.

## Acceptance criteria

- [AC-1] Inbound links in the rail's rows are buttons that open the linked artifact, not inert
  text.
- [AC-2] PlanTab's discard-unsaved prompt is the application's own dialog in the established
  Radix idiom; no `window.confirm` remains in the renderer.
- [AC-3] The dead props are gone: `NowTab.onPrompt` and `ReviewTab.onOpen` no longer exist.
- [AC-4] The two commit actions read "Commit staged" and "Commit all changes"; the review's two
  send actions read "Send back to build session" and "Hand to main chat".
- [AC-5] FileTab's held dot carries a tooltip that says what being held means.
