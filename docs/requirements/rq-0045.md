# RQ-0045 — Three surfaces, no nested strips

The center panel's pinned tabs become three surfaces named for what a person is doing — **Chat,
Plan, Work** — and no surface hides a second tab strip inside itself. Plan absorbs PlanTab, so a
drafted plan is something you browse to rather than something a conditional banner may or may not
offer. Work is the board plus the sprint header. And the Build split button flips to match how
building actually happens now: parallel worktree builds are the default, the conversation is the
exception ([DC-0027](../decisions/dc-0027.md); owner decision).

## Acceptance criteria

- [AC-1] The pinned tabs are exactly Chat, Plan, and Work; no pinned surface renders a nested
  tab strip.
- [AC-2] The Plan surface absorbs PlanTab: browsing to Plan shows any drafted plan rendered
  in-surface, with its review and edit affordances; the conditional banner — previously the
  plan's only entry — is gone.
- [AC-3] The Work surface is the work board with the sprint header.
- [AC-4] The Build split button's primary action is the worktree build; "In this conversation"
  sits behind the caret.
- [AC-5] Document tabs — files, sessions, plans, reviews, diffs — keep their behaviour and their
  preview-tab semantics unchanged.
