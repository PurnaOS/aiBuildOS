# RQ-0015 — A story is built by the agent and accepted by a person

The last arc of the loop: a ready story is handed to the agent, and what comes back is put in
front of a person, beside what was asked for, to accept or send back. The vision doc is explicit
that nothing becomes part of the product without this okay — and equally explicit about what the
okay is here: a state flip in the record, `review → accepted`, made by a human hand through the
guarded save path.

The walk is `ready → queued → building → review`. Starting the build writes the first flips and
runs the build playbook with the story and its requirement as context; the end of the turn is
when the work stops moving, which is when it goes to `review`. The review surface owes the
reviewer one thing above all: the story's criteria and the working tree's diffs on one screen, so
"does it do what was asked" is a comparison, not an act of memory.

Git stays untouched. The changes stay in the working tree the Git tab already shows; committing
is its own requirement with its own failure modes. And because every state lives in the record,
an application restart loses the conversation but not the process: the story is still at
`review`, still waiting for its verdict.

## Acceptance criteria

- [AC-1] A ready story offers building; starting it flips the story to `building` (through
  `queued`) and runs the build playbook with the story and its requirement as context.
- [AC-2] While the build turn runs, the story's card and the conversation both show it as
  building.
- [AC-3] When the turn ends, the story moves to `review`, and the review surface shows the
  story's acceptance criteria and the working tree's changes as diffs, side by side.
- [AC-4] Accepting flips `review → accepted`; sending back returns it to `building` with the
  reviewer's note appended to the story and sent to the agent.
- [AC-5] Nothing here writes to Git; the changes stay in the working tree the Git tab shows.
- [AC-6] The story's state and the review verdict survive a restart, because they are the
  record; the conversation need not.
