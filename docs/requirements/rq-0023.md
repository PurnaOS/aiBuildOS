# RQ-0023 — A manual check is a guided checklist, and its outcome is recorded

The profile has said `kind: manual | automated` from the beginning, and every test case so far is
automated because a manual one had nowhere to run. The vision doc's step 9 names the other half:
simple, guided checklists that walk a person through checking something by hand — with the
outcome landing in the record, because a check nobody wrote down is a check that didn't happen.

The outcome is three optional TestCase fields — `last_result`, `last_run`, `last_run_by` — written
through the guarded save like every other record edit. Fields, not states: a test case's state
still only says whether it is active, and gates that read results arrive with their own
requirement.

## Acceptance criteria

- [AC-1] A manual test case's Steps render as a checklist a person walks through in order, from
  the story it verifies.
- [AC-2] Completing a walk records pass or fail, when, and by whom, in the test case's own
  fields, through the guarded save; abandoning a walk records nothing.
- [AC-3] A story under review shows its manual checks' latest outcomes beside the automated
  checks' run.
- [AC-4] The profile declares the outcome fields; a bundle without them stays valid — they are
  optional, and their absence means "never walked".
