# RQ-0029 — The workspace stays scannable and honest at scale

Seventy artifacts in, the record rail is one unbroken list under static headers: no counts, no
folding, and the filter hiding at the bottom of the pane. The review that diagnosed it also
ranked the chrome's other rough edges — a native `window.confirm` blocking the renderer in the
platform's own styling, five different bare "Loading…" strings, and one icon shared by every
kind of tab. They ride together: this is the requirement that the furniture keeps up.

## Acceptance criteria

- [AC-1] The rail's type groups fold and unfold, each header a control carrying the group's name
  and count in words; folding survives a refresh, and the filter still searches folded groups.
- [AC-2] The filter sits at the top of the rail, shows how many artifacts match, and clears in
  one press.
- [AC-3] The discard-unsaved prompt is the application's own dialog, in its own styling, in both
  appearances.
- [AC-4] Now, session, plan, review and diff tabs each carry an icon that says what they are;
  loading states share one quiet treatment instead of five strings.
