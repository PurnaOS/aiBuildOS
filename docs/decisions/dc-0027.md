# DC-0027 — The activity dock replaces Now; three surfaces

## Context

The owner's second real use produced two complaints that turned out to be one: "I'm not sure what
the Now tab is for" and "the center panel is too busy, with no flow — too much tab switching".
The diagnosis is structural, not cosmetic. Now's only data source was the worktree build list,
while the default build path ran in the main chat — so the tab that promised "what is happening"
sat empty exactly when things were happening, and the owner's confusion was the correct reading
of what the screen showed. Meanwhile activity that did exist was findable only by visiting the
right tab at the right moment, and the pinned strip mixed surfaces, boards-within-tabs, and a
tab whose emptiness was a bug of information architecture.

## Decision

**Three pinned surfaces, named for what a person is doing: Chat, Plan, Work.** Plan absorbs
PlanTab so drafts are reached by browsing, not by a conditional banner; Work is the board plus
the sprint header; no surface nests a second tab strip.

**A persistent activity dock replaces Now.** A collapsible strip at the bottom of the center
panel, visible from every surface — activity stops living in a tab you must visit. Collapsed
(28px) it shows one motion line, an amber "N needs you" rollup, and a next-action line;
expanded it is resizable, with sections for builds, the main chat, background runs, and
terminals.

**Derivation is from the session inventory**, not `build:list` — `deriveDock(sessions, builds,
backgroundRuns, terminals)`. This is the fix for Now's structural emptiness: whatever kind of
session is doing the work, the dock sees it. The needs-you rollup therefore includes the main
chat waiting on the user, worktree permission requests, and blocked background runs, answered
through one extracted `PermissionCard` instead of three duplicated renderings.

**The next-action line is the stage indicator — deliberately no stepper.** "ST-0042 waits in
review → [Review]" tells the user where the flow stands *and* is the button that advances it.
A pipeline picture would restate the same fact without the press.

## Consequences

- Now dies; its row derivation becomes `deriveDock`, and its testids (`now-row-*`, `now-badge`)
  keep their names on their new home, so specs migrate by relocation, not rewrite.
- Every surface shows the same dock, so "did it land in Chat?" stops being a question — the main
  chat's turn activity is in the dock from anywhere.
- The dock is the natural home for what later work surfaces: worktree rows with Resume, terminal
  sessions, background runs. New activity kinds join a section instead of minting a tab.
- 28px of every surface is permanently spent on the dock; the price of glanceability, accepted.

## Alternatives considered

- **Keep Now as a tab and fix its data source**: a fuller tab is still a tab — activity remains
  invisible from every other surface, and "you must visit to notice" was the failure being
  fixed, not the rendering inside it.
- **A stage-stepper widget**: implies a linear pipeline the work does not have (parallel builds,
  background runs, chat work all interleave), and it depicts the state without offering the
  action. The next-action line carries the same information as a button.
- **An auto-hiding dock**: motion you cannot glance at, and appearing/vanishing chrome is churn.
  The collapsed strip costs 28px; stillness is worth more than the pixels.
