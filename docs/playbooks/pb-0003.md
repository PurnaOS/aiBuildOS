# PB-0003 — Build a story

I will name one Story. Read it, the Requirement it implements, and its acceptance criteria in full
before writing anything.

Implement the story and nothing beyond it — no criterion invented, none skipped. When you are done,
tell me which criteria you believe are met and which are not, so review checks your work against the
story's own words rather than your account of them.

If you are building in a worktree, leave every `state:` field exactly as you found it — the
application walks the story's states, not you (DC-0021).
