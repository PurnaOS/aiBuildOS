# EP-0015 — Design before build

Between a clear requirement and an approved plan there is nothing to look at, and for anything
with a face that gap is where the expensive misunderstandings live. The market has three answers —
mockup-first generation, visual editing on live code, and a Figma handoff; the owner chose
mockup-first, in-app (decision recorded 2026-08-21): the agent proposes a rendered design from the
requirement, the owner approves it beside the requirement, and only then does planning carry it
into the build. `Design` enters the profile as its own type, the Sprint precedent.

## Scope

### In scope

- The `Design` type — `DS`, `docs/designs/`, `draft → review → approved`
  ([the Design profile](../profile/design.md)).
- Agent-proposed mockups rendered in the existing preview surface, the approve/revise loop beside
  the requirement, and handoff into plan and build ([RQ-0048](../requirements/rq-0048.md)).

### Out of scope

- **Figma MCP handoff** — real and bidirectional in the market, but beta, seat-gated, and aimed at
  teams with designers. Named here so it is not mistaken for an oversight; it gets its own
  requirement when wanted.
- **Visual editing on the preview** (the Onlook shape) — the largest build for the smallest
  slice of our audience, today.
- **Design-system import** — grounding mockups in an existing component library follows once
  designs exist at all.
