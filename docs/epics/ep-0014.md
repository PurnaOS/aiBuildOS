# EP-0014 — Ship it

Competitive research (2026-08-21) said one thing loudest: every product a non-engineer would
compare us to ends at a live URL — one-click hosting, a stable link, publish again and the link
still works. aiBuildOS ends at accept → commit; [EP-0011](ep-0011.md) got the code as far as the
owner's own remote, and there the journey stops. This epic finishes it: publish from inside the
application, and make what shipped a readable part of the record.

## Scope

### In scope

- Publishing accepted work to a live URL as an agent-driven playbook through a provider's CLI,
  the URL surfaced in the workspace ([RQ-0046](../requirements/rq-0046.md)).
- Releases as record artifacts — when, the commit, the URL, the stories, agent-drafted notes
  ([RQ-0047](../requirements/rq-0047.md)).

### Out of scope

- **Hosting of our own** — the application composes providers; it does not become one.
- **Managed auth, databases, payments** — the platform-builder half of the market's offer.
  Revisit with starter templates (already an open item in
  [decisions](../decisions/README.md#not-yet-decided)).
- **Auto-publish** — EP-0011's rule holds: every sync is its own press, and so is every publish.
