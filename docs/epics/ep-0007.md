# EP-0007 — The conversation asks and listens

The vision doc's front door (steps 1–3) and its manner of speaking (step 8): a question from the
agent arrives as a choice, not a paragraph, and an idea arrives as an interview that leaves draft
requirements behind. [EP-0006](ep-0006.md) closed the loop for work already written down; this
epic is how work gets written down by talking.

The mechanism is [DC-0020](../decisions/dc-0020.md)'s fenced-block convention — deliberately a
convention, deliberately degradable to plain text.

## Scope

### In scope

- The question card: a completed message's fenced question rendered as tappable options, free
  text always beside them, malformed structure falling back to visible text.
- The intake playbook and its interview: one question per turn, draft requirements written by the
  agent as they are settled, appearing on the backlog as they land.
- Abandonable, re-enterable: drafts written stay drafts; nothing obliges building them.

### Out of scope

- **Supervision levels** — step 8's other half arrives with its own requirement, later in the
  roadmap.
- **ACP elicitation** — the migration DC-0020 names, when a harness ships it.
- **Attachments and sketches** as intake material — text and paste first.
