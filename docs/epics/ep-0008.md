# EP-0008 — Proving it works

The vision doc's step 9: quality checked automatically, results always visible. Review today
([EP-0006](ep-0006.md)) reads diffs against criteria; this epic gives it evidence — the project's
own checks, run from the workspace, their outcome standing beside the story they judge.

## Scope

### In scope

- The project's check commands, declared in the record and run as child processes in main, output
  visible as it arrives, exit codes the only verdict.
- The outcome in words beside the story under review, and a one-button handoff of a failure to
  the agent.

### Out of scope

- **Gates** — refusing a state flip until checks pass is its own deferred requirement; v1 tells
  the truth without blocking anyone.
- **Manual QA checklists** and **previews** — later requirements in this epic's territory.
- **Per-TestCase `binding` runs and recorded results** — the smallest loop runs the project's
  declared checks; recording results into TestCase artifacts arrives with the gates that need it.
